Dwight Sheets

A personal automation tool

Dwight Sheets is a private, personal automation tool. It is used only by its owner, Samuel Wong (GitHub: im-samwong), to work with his own Google Drive, Google Sheets, Gmail and Google Calendar.

What it does

  • Creates and updates spreadsheets in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Reads the owner's own email and prepares drafts for him to review.
  • Reads and updates events in the owner's own calendar.

Who can use it

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access anyone else's Google account.
